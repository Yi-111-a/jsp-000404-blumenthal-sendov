## What the headline covers

Sendov's determination of α_N (Blumenthal minimax angle): for integers n≥2, α_N = π(1−1/n) when 2^{n−1}+2^{n−3} < N ≤ 2^n, and α_N = π(1−1/(2n−1)) when 2^{n−1} < N ≤ 2^{n−1}+2^{n−3}.
